02 — MY ENTREPRENEURIAL VENTURE

Introducing KasiBook

A digital business concept designed around the needs of township entrepreneurs.

BUSINESS CONCEPT

Bringing Local Businesses Online.

KasiBook is a booking and ordering platform concept designed to help township businesses connect with customers through a convenient digital platform.

The idea focuses on businesses such as salons, barbershops and kota vendors. Customers can discover services, make bookings and place orders, while business owners can manage their customer interactions more conveniently.

01

The Problem

Some local businesses rely on manual booking and ordering processes, which can make customer management difficult.

02

The Solution

A central digital platform that helps customers interact with participating township businesses.

03

Target Market

Township entrepreneurs, including salons, barbershops, food vendors and their customers.
