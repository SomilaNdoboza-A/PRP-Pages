ARTEFACT 02 / REQUIREMENTS

User Stories & Tasks

The user stories describe the functionality the team planned to deliver, organised into the first four development sprints.

Purpose of the user stories

User stories translated the KasiBook product idea into smaller pieces of work from the perspective of the people who would use the platform. The supplied user-story plan covered Sprints 1–4, with four story groups per sprint (US-1.1 to US-4.4).

Sprint 1 — Foundation and discovery

STORYFOCUSWORK REPRESENTED
US-1.1Project setupEstablish the project structure and development foundation.
US-1.2UI/UX wireframingPlan the main screens and user journeys before implementation.
US-1.3Target audience and contextUnderstand the needs of township businesses and their customers.
US-1.4Progress and log syncKeep team progress and project records updated.

Reflection

Writing the requirements as user stories helped the team break a broad idea into work that could be planned and discussed. I learned that a feature such as booking is not one isolated screen: it involves the customer’s choices, date and time information, confirmation and the data that the business needs to manage the appointment. The story groups also helped me understand where my backend and database responsibilities connected to the frontend work. In future, I would make sure every story has clear acceptance criteria and that the detailed tasks remain linked to the story throughout development.
