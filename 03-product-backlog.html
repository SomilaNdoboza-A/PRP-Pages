ARTEFACT 03 / PRIORITISED WORK

Product Backlog

The backlog records the planned scope and priority order for the KasiBook product, including what was complete and what remained planned at the recorded review point.

Backlog overview

The product backlog is the ordered list of work planned for KasiBook. The recorded backlog contains 23 items across six planned sprints. Items 1–16 were marked complete on the Trello board snapshot dated 29 October 2026; items 17–23 were still marked planned in that snapshot.

Prioritisation approach: The sequence begins with setup, research and design, moves to core booking and frontend features, then schedules roles, notifications, further design, integration and testing.

Reflection

The product backlog gave the team one shared view of the work instead of relying on separate personal task lists. I learned why ordering matters: the team needed to establish the project and understand the users before building the booking experience, and the frontend needed a structure before more complex functionality could be connected. I also learned that a backlog changes as a project develops. The archived security item is a reminder that when work is moved or rewritten, the reason and the related replacement work should remain clear so that important requirements are not lost.
