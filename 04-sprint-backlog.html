Reflection

The sprint plan helped me see how development work is delivered in stages and how one sprint’s output becomes a dependency for later work. The final development period was especially important because the team had to bring the frontend, backend functionality and database work together rather than treating them as separate pieces. Testing was part of finishing the work, not something to leave until after the project. I also learned to keep the portfolio honest about project records: planned work, completed work and work that still needs evidence must be distinguished clearly.
